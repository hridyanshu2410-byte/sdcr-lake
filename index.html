sdcr-lake

Data pipeline for the San Diego Housing Command Room research project

sdcr-lake is a private research tool operated by Hridyanshu, a student researcher at the University of California San Diego. It collects publicly available datasets about San Diego housing, land use, construction, transportation and related topics from government open-data portals, and stores copies of those datasets in the operator's own Google Drive so that they can be analyzed later.

How it uses Google

The tool signs in to a single Google account, the operator's own, through Google's standard OAuth consent screen. It uses the Google Drive API only to create, update and list the dataset files it stores in a dedicated folder of that account's Drive. It does not read, change or share any other files, and it is not offered to other users.
